# Stable greeting portrait
Built-in imagegen edit of ash-talking-v2.png using the user's WhatsApp portrait as the beauty-mark reference.
Output: public/assets/character/ash-greeting-v3.png.
Prompt: One transparent chest-up portrait, same illustrated woman, black hijab and sweatshirt, friendly softly parted smile. Match the photograph's tiny brown beauty mark above the middle/outer-middle of her right eyebrow, preserving relative spacing from eyebrow and hijab. No sprite grid, no text, no background.
Animation uses a single registered portrait with a feathered lip-only deformation. No body or head transforms and no mismatched sprite frames.
