# Full-body greeting
Built-in imagegen, based on approved option A. Asset: public/assets/character/ash-fullbody-greeting.png.
Prompt: Two registered full-body cells, same facial likeness and cel-shaded artwork, black hijab and sweatshirt, grey sweatpants and white sneakers. Left open-palm wave, right one-eye wink and one-handed V sign, same body alignment and fixed smile, transparent background.
The body always uses the first cell. Only the raised hand rotates during typing; it changes to the aligned victory hand after typing. Only the eye region switches during the wink. Stars originate at the winking eye. Mouth stays still. Reduced motion disables hand rotation and particles.
