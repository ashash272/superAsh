# Astronomy background

Created with the built-in image-generation tool. Integrated artwork: public/assets/environments/astronomy-sky.png. Drifting planets, shooting stars and twinkles are animated in the application.

Prompt: Premium high-resolution illustrated outer-space backdrop for a polished 2D portfolio game. Wide landscape composition. Deep midnight indigo, luminous teal and violet nebula clouds sweeping diagonally like the Milky Way, densely layered tiny distant stars, atmospheric cosmic depth, delicate warm gold light. Lower quarter dark and quiet to support overlaid playable sports courts. Upper middle calm enough for UI readability. Beautiful richly detailed painterly astronomical concept art, not pixel art, not geometric flat gradients. NO planets, NO large foreground stars, NO meteors: these will be animated separately in code. No land, trees, buildings, text, borders, people or interface. Full bleed artwork.
